What we fix

  • Route listed as "gone"
    GET/HEAD returns 404 while POST returns 402, so indexers and health checks drop your route.
  • Template paths being charged
    Literal placeholders like /:id or {symbol} are listed and charged as-is.
  • Testnet-only accepts
    Your 402 only offers Base Sepolia, so real buyers on Base mainnet cannot pay.
  • Version mismatch
    The 402 body says x402 v1 while the header says v2 (or the reverse), and clients reject it.
  • 400/401 before 402
    Validation or auth runs before the payment challenge, so agents never see the price.
  • Listing issues
    Missing or broken entries in the CDP Bazaar, 402index or agentexchange: wrong metadata, stale routes, no discovery info.

Free self-check

Run this against one of your paid routes. A healthy x402 route answers 402 to both GET and POST (or at least not 404 on GET/HEAD), and offers Base mainnet (eip155:8453 or base).

URL="https://your-api.example.com/your/paid/route"
for m in GET HEAD POST; do
  printf "%-5s " $m; curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" -X $m "$URL"
done
curl -s -D - -o /tmp/402.json "$URL" | grep -i "payment-required\|x-payment" | cut -c1-120
grep -o '"x402Version":[0-9]*\|"network":"[^"]*"' /tmp/402.json | sort -u
